What we do

For every match we cover, ScoreOcs8 produces a prediction: a pick (home / draw / away), a confidence level, a projected scoreline, and a short, plain-English explanation of why. We also surface the recent head-to-head record, venue and kickoff time so you can judge the call yourself. Predictions are published before kickoff and the final result is shown afterwards.

How our predictions are made

Our picks combine an AI analysis layer with a data-driven form model. The model weighs recent head-to-head meetings, home advantage, goals scored and conceded, and the stage of the competition. When a deeper AI write-up is available it leads; otherwise the form model produces an honest, data-only preview. We label which is which. We don't dress a form lean up as something it isn't.

Predictions are opinions, not certainties. Football is unpredictable, and we say so on every card. Confidence figures describe how clear-cut a matchup looks, not a promise of the outcome.

We log outcomes publicly, wins and losses. Each finished match shows whether our pick landed, with the final score. We believe a prediction site is only worth following if it shows its misses too, so we don't hide them.
