## How to read this (important for posting)

- All kick-off times below are **Malaysia Time (MYT / UTC+8)**.
- Conversion rule used: **MYT = US Eastern Time (ET) + 12 hours.** Because of the +12h jump,
  almost every match lands on the **next calendar day in Malaysia**, in the early-morning to
  midday window. The MYT date shown is the Malaysian date.
- **Start vs. End:** A match has no fixed end time. As a rule of thumb:
  - **Group / regular 90-min matches:** finish **≈ 1h 50m – 2h** after kick-off
    (90 min + 15 min half-time + stoppage). → *End ≈ start + 2 hours.*
  - **Knockout matches that go to extra time + penalties:** can run up to
    **≈ 2h 45m – 3h.** → *End ≈ start + 3 hours.*
- ⚠️ Knockout-round **match-ups are placeholders** (Winner Group A, Runner-up B, Best 3rd, etc.)
  until the group stage finishes. Dates/times are fixed; the teams fill in as results come.
- Times are compiled from major outlets (FIFA, ESPN, CBS, Sky, Al Jazeera, NBC). FIFA can tweak
  kick-off times slightly — re-confirm marquee games against fifa.com close to match day.

**Typical Malaysian viewing windows:** 12:00am, 1–6am, 7–12pm MYT depending on US time zone.
