Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 June 2026
ScoreOcs8 is a football prediction and analysis website. We do not run betting accounts, take wagers, or hold funds. There is no sign-up on ScoreOcs8 — so we collect very little about you, and we never sell your data.

1. Who we are

ScoreOcs8 ("we", "us") publishes football match previews, AI- and community-driven predictions, fixtures, scores and analysis. Scores and odds shown on the site are context — they indicate which team our models and community favour. They are not betting advice and we are not a gambling operator.

2. Information we collect

Because there is no account system, we collect only what is needed to run the site:

  • Technical logs. Like most websites, our host (Cloudflare) records standard request data such as IP address, browser/user-agent and pages requested, for security, abuse-prevention and performance. This is aggregated and short-lived.
  • On-device storage. Your match-market votes, theme and other preferences are stored in your browser's local storage. They stay on your device and are not tied to your identity.
  • Push notifications (optional). If you opt in to browser notifications, we store an anonymous push subscription token so we can send match alerts. You can revoke this any time in your browser settings.

We do not collect your name, email or payment details, because you do not create an account or transact with us.

3. Cookies & local storage

We use a small amount of local storage and essential cookies to remember your preferences and keep the site working. We do not use cross-site advertising trackers. Our host (Cloudflare) may set security/performance cookies. You can clear or block these in your browser; some features (such as remembering your votes) may then not work.

4. Third parties

  • Cloudflare — hosting, CDN and security for the site.
  • Sports data providers — we fetch fixtures, scores and stats from football data APIs. We send no personal data to them.
  • OCS8 Sports (third-party operator). When you click a link to OCS8 Sports you leave ScoreOcs8 and enter a separate company's website, governed by their terms and privacy policy. We may pass a referral code so they know you came from us. We do not control their site or handle any betting or payment data.
  • Social channels. If you follow us on Telegram or other platforms, those platforms' own privacy policies apply.

5. Your choices & rights

You can clear local storage, disable cookies, and turn off push notifications at any time from your browser. If you are in a jurisdiction with data-protection rights (for example Malaysia's PDPA), you may contact us about the limited technical data we hold. Reach us via our Telegram channel: t.me/scoreocs8.

6. Children

ScoreOcs8 is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children.

7. Changes

We may update this policy as the site evolves. We will revise the "last updated" date above when we do.
